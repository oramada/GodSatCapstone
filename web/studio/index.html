    Initial states

    Generated constellations are hypothetical circular initial states. No measured orbit or observation history is implied.
    

    Time & resolution
    Cadence must divide duration and be a multiple of the integration step. Smaller steps should be checked for convergence.
    

    Force models
    Constant-area solar-pressure model; spherical Earth shadow. Gravity is zonal only. These are optional approximations, not full orbit determination.
    

    Spacecraft properties
    Effective areas and coefficients are inputs. They are not inferred from the displayed spacecraft shape.
    

    Atmosphere inputs
    Drag uses local F10.7 and Ap history. Validation checks coverage for the entire run; missing drivers are never filled automatically. Displayed clouds, magnetic and ionospheric layers are not drag inputs.
